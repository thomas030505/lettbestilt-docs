---
title: Payments
subtitle: Online payment through a hosted page, or cash
---

Check `restaurant.capabilities.payment` to see what the restaurant accepts.

## Online payment

Create the order with `paymentMethod: "STRIPE"`. The response includes `stripeUrl`, a hosted payment page run by SagaPay. The customer chooses the method there: card, Vipps, and any other method the restaurant has enabled with SagaPay, such as Klarna.

1. Create the order with `successUrl` and `cancelUrl`.
2. Redirect the customer to `stripeUrl`.
3. After payment, the customer returns to `successUrl`. Put `{TOKEN}` in the URL to get the order's `publicToken` back.
4. If the customer cancels, they return to `cancelUrl`.

The order counts as paid when LettBestilt receives the payment confirmation. Do not treat the redirect to `successUrl` alone as proof of payment. Check the order status, or listen for the `ORDER_PAID` [webhook](/webhooks).

<Note>
`stripeUrl` is a historical name. Payments run through SagaPay, and Vipps is chosen inside the hosted page. Do not use the legacy `vippsUrl` field in new integrations.
</Note>

## Cash

With `paymentMethod: "CASH"` there is no payment link. Show a confirmation right away; the customer pays at pickup or delivery.

## Return URLs

`successUrl` and `cancelUrl` must point to the restaurant's verified domain or to LettBestilt. Other domains are rejected with `INVALID_SUCCESS_URL` or `INVALID_CANCEL_URL`.
