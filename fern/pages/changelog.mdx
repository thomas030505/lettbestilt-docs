## 2026-10-20: Set up a restaurant through the API, and LettBestilt MCP configuration tools

**Only additive changes in `/api/v1`.** No endpoint, field or event was removed or renamed.

### New

- **[Set up a restaurant through the API](/partner-setup-api)**: the partner API now sets up the restaurant itself, with the same rules as the restaurant dashboard. A partner can change what a developer on the partner team can change there, and nothing more.
  - **Settings**: `GET` and `PATCH …/{slug}/settings` for the profile, visiting address, colours, logo and cover image, pickup and delivery, preparation times, the texts on the tracking page and the receipt, and the legal fields. `version` and `expectedVersion` stop you from overwriting a change made in between.
  - **Opening hours**: `GET` and `PUT …/{slug}/hours` for the week, and `POST …/{slug}/hours/overrides`, `PATCH` and `DELETE …/{slug}/hours/overrides/{overrideId}` for holidays and other exceptions.
  - **Delivery zones**: `GET` and `POST …/{slug}/delivery-zones`, `PATCH` and `DELETE …/{slug}/delivery-zones/{zoneId}`, by postal code or by radius.
  - **Menus**: `POST …/{slug}/menus` creates a menu that is turned off from the same proposal as menu import, `PATCH …/{slug}/menus/{menuId}` publishes it, `PUT …/{slug}/menus/{menuId}/schedules` sets its schedule, and `GET`, `DELETE` read and remove menus.
  - **Categories, products and addon groups**: `POST …/{slug}/menus/{menuId}/categories`, `PATCH` and `DELETE …/{slug}/categories/{categoryId}`, `POST …/{slug}/categories/{categoryId}/products`, `POST …/{slug}/products/batch` (up to 50 products in one call), `PATCH` and `DELETE …/{slug}/products/{productId}`, and `GET`, `POST`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` for `…/{slug}/addon-groups`. Sizes and addons keep their ids when you send them back, so baskets on the website keep working.
- **[LettBestilt MCP](/mcp)** now sets up a whole restaurant, with a new class of **configuration** tools.
  - **Opening hours, delivery and settings**: `set_opening_hours`, `add_hours_override`, `update_hours_override`, `create_delivery_zone`, `update_delivery_zone` and `update_settings`, plus the read tools `get_opening_hours`, `list_delivery_zones` and `get_settings`.
  - **The menu**: `create_menu` creates a menu that is turned off from a proposal the AI tool builds from the restaurant's menu, `add_category`, `update_category`, `add_products`, `update_product` and `set_addon_group` edit it piece by piece (sizes and addons keep their ids), `set_menu_schedule` sets its schedule, and `publish_menu` puts it in service in one of [three ways](/mcp#publish-a-menu). The read tool `list_menus` lists every menu, and `get_menu` with `menuId` reads one menu, also when it is off.
  - They call the partner API, so they validate and fail the same way. They never delete a menu, category, product, addon group, delivery zone or override: those are turned off with `isActive: false` or ended with `endDate`. They never remove a size or an addon either: `update_product` and `set_addon_group` send the whole set with the ids, and a set that leaves out an existing size or addon gives `422 VALIDATION_FAILED`; a person removes it in the dashboard or through the partner API. `publish_menu`, `set_menu_schedule`, `update_category`, `update_product` and `set_addon_group` are marked `destructiveHint`, so AI tools ask before they run them.
  - The guide [Set up a restaurant from scratch](/mcp#set-up-a-restaurant-from-scratch) walks through the order: sandbox, settings, hours, delivery, menu, domains and webhooks, test order and go-live.
- **A welcome card and `help` in LettBestilt MCP**: when you greet the AI tool or ask what it can do, it shows what it can do and a few things to try, and "hjelp" or "help" gives every tool, one area ("hjelp meny") or one tool with its fields and an example ("hjelp publish_menu"), in Norwegian or English. Claude Code also gets the prompts `/mcp__lettbestilt__welcome` and `/mcp__lettbestilt__help`, where `help` takes `language` (`nb` or `en`). See [Getting started and help](/mcp#getting-started-and-help).
- **`create_sandbox`** in LettBestilt MCP creates a sandbox with a test partner key. It never returns a restaurant key; a person creates it in LettBestilt Partner.
- **[Your own partner account](/partner-setup-api#your-own-partner-account) through the API**, with only the partner key: `GET /me`, `GET` and `PATCH /brand` (also the light logo, and with white label the colours and font of your restaurant owners' dashboard), `POST /brand/logo` and `POST /brand/logo-light`, the sender domain (`GET`, `POST`, `DELETE /brand/sender-domain` and `POST …/verify`), `GET` and `PATCH /profile`, and news to your restaurants (`GET` and `POST /news`, `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /news/{newsId}`, `POST /news/{newsId}/publish`). Reading needs no role; changes need a key created by an owner or administrator (`403 KEY_ROLE_FORBIDDEN`).
- **LettBestilt MCP sets up your partner account** with 12 new tools, 52 in all: seven in a new class, **partner account**, and five read tools: `get_partner`, `get_partner_brand`, `set_partner_brand` (with a logo and a light logo up to 512 KB each, and with white label the dashboard colours and font), `get_partner_profile`, `update_partner_profile`, `get_sender_domain`, `set_sender_domain`, `check_sender_domain`, `list_partner_news`, `create_partner_news`, `update_partner_news` and `publish_partner_news`. They work with test and live partner keys, and the tools that change something have their own limit of 60 changes per hour per partner key. They never remove the logos (a new one replaces it) or the sender domain, never delete or archive news, never set your website, and never publish news to real restaurants: with a live key the AI tool writes the draft and a person publishes it (`403 HUMAN_REQUIRED`). See [Your partner account](/mcp#your-partner-account).

### Changed

- **Menu `priority`**: `PATCH …/menus/{menuId}` takes `priority` (0–100), and `GET …/menus`, `list_menus` and `publish_menu` show and take it. When several menus have a time window at the same moment, the highest priority is shown. A menu that replaces the main menu for a period needs dates, a full-week schedule **and** a priority higher than the main menu's; see [Publish the menu](/partner-setup-api#6-publish-the-menu).
- `POST …/menus` and committing a menu import count the proposal's products against the product limit (`409 PRODUCT_LIMIT`), and `PATCH …/menus/{menuId}` cleans `name` (`textCleaned`).
- `PUT …/menus/{menuId}/schedules` rejects unknown fields on a row with `422`.
- A restaurant has at most 200 opening hours overrides (`409 HOURS_OVERRIDE_LIMIT`).
- Addon groups: `minSelect` above the number of available addons gives `422`, `isRequired` must agree with `minSelect`, and a required `GLOBAL` group comes back with a warning.
- The partner's 30 changes per minute are counted per environment, so testing with test keys never slows down changes to a real restaurant.
- `PATCH /news/{newsId}` with an empty `restaurantSlugs` on news that is not for all restaurants gives `422`.
- Free text in settings, delivery zones and opening hours overrides is cleaned like menu text, with `textCleaned` in the response when something was removed.
- The legal fields are locked after the owner has signed the customer agreement in the restaurant dashboard too (`LEGAL_LOCKED`).
- The go-live check has a new point, `delivery`: a warning when delivery is on without an active delivery zone.
- LettBestilt MCP accepts requests up to 1 MB (was 64 KB), so a menu of a couple of hundred products, or a logo, fits in one call.
- LettBestilt MCP's configuration tools have their own limit of 300 changes per hour per partner key, separate from the 60 per hour for setup tools. `RATE_LIMITED` from these limits and from the partner API carries `details.retryAfterSeconds`.
- New error codes: `DEMO_RESTAURANT`, `LEGAL_LOCKED`, `HOURS_OVERRIDE_NOT_FOUND`, `DELIVERY_ZONE_NOT_FOUND`, `DELIVERY_ZONE_LIMIT`, `HOURS_OVERRIDE_LIMIT`, `MENU_NOT_FOUND`, `MENU_IS_DEFAULT`, `CATEGORY_NOT_FOUND`, `PRODUCT_NOT_FOUND`, `ADDON_GROUP_NOT_FOUND`, `CATEGORY_NOT_EMPTY`, `CATEGORY_LIMIT`, `PRODUCT_LIMIT` and `ADDON_GROUP_LIMIT`, `SANDBOX_TEST_KEY_REQUIRED` in LettBestilt MCP, and for your own account `KEY_ROLE_FORBIDDEN`, `WHITE_LABEL_REQUIRED`, `HUMAN_REQUIRED`, `LENGTH_REQUIRED`, `LOGO_NOT_ALLOWED`, `FILE_TOO_LARGE`, `UPLOAD_FAILED`, `PARTNER_ARCHIVED`, `PARTNER_NEWS_FORBIDDEN`, `NEWS_NOT_FOUND`, `NOT_DRAFT`, `NOT_PUBLISHED`, `NOT_SCHEDULED`, `NOT_CLAIMABLE`, `NO_RECIPIENTS`, `IMAGE_NOT_ALLOWED`, `SENDER_DOMAIN_ALREADY_SET`, `SENDER_DOMAIN_NOT_SET`, `DOMAIN_EXISTS`, `SENDER_DOMAIN_REJECTED` and `EMAIL_SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE`. See [Errors](/errors).

## 2026-10-19: Domains, owner invitations, go-live approval, menu import and LettBestilt MCP

**Only additive changes in `/api/v1`.** No endpoint, field or event was removed or renamed.

### New

- **[Domains](/partner-api#domains)** in the LettBestilt Partner API: add a domain, get the TXT record, verify it yourself and choose the domain for tracking links. The same is on the restaurant's **Domains** tab in LettBestilt Partner (the partner portal). LettBestilt no longer has to verify domains for you.
- **[Owner invitations](/partner-api#inviting-the-owner)**: `POST` and `DELETE …/owner-invite` invite a real restaurant's owner by email. The owner creates their own login, signs the agreements and sets the restaurant live.
- **[Go-live approval](/partner-api#go-live-approval)**: a real restaurant needs LettBestilt's approval before it goes live for the first time. The owner signs the agreements. LettBestilt approves the restaurant before the owner sets it live. Ask for it in the partner portal, or with `POST …/go-live-review`. `GET /partner/restaurants/{slug}` has new `owner` and `goLive` objects.
- **[Menu import](/partner-api#menu-import)**: upload a menu as a PDF or photo, check the proposal and import it as a new menu that is turned off.
- **[LettBestilt MCP](/mcp)**, the MCP server at `https://www.lettbestilt.no/api/mcp` for AI assistants such as Claude Code and Cursor. With the partner key, an assistant can read a restaurant's setup, create webhooks and domains, ask for go-live approval and place test orders in a sandbox. It cannot touch orders on real restaurants, customer data or keys, and it cannot delete anything. Until live partner keys are available, it works with a test partner key against sandboxes.
- **Partner team roles.** Test partner keys can be created by the owner, administrators and developers on your partner team; live partner keys only by the owner and administrators.

### Changed

- Owner invitations and go-live requests are for real restaurants, so through the API they need a live partner key. Live partner keys are still not available; until then, use the partner portal.
- A partner account that registered itself gets `403 PARTNER_NOT_APPROVED` from the partner API until LettBestilt has approved it.
- Webhooks for a real restaurant can only be added once its owner has signed the customer agreement and the data processing agreement (`403 PARTNER_DATA_LOCKED`).
- New error codes: `PARTNER_NOT_APPROVED`, `PARTNER_DATA_LOCKED`, `SANDBOX_NOT_SUPPORTED`, `DOMAIN_NOT_FOUND`, `DOMAIN_TAKEN`, `DOMAIN_LIMIT`, `DOMAIN_MANAGED`, `DOMAIN_NOT_VERIFIED`, `OWNER_EXISTS`, `OWNER_EMAIL_NOT_ALLOWED`, `OWNER_INVITE_NOT_FOUND`, `INVITE_SEND_FAILED`, the `GO_LIVE_…` codes, the menu import codes (`PDF_…`, `IMAGE_…`, `FILE_UNSUPPORTED` and `MENU_IMPORT_…`), and `NO_TABLE` and `INVALID_PARTY_SIZE`, which are reserved. The error code list in the API reference is now complete, including the reservation codes. See [Errors](/errors).

## 2026-10-16: Campaign tracking

### New

- **[Campaign tracking](/campaign-tracking).** A script for the restaurant's website, `https://www.lettbestilt.no/sporing/v1.js`, remembers the newsletter campaign a subscriber clicked for 30 days and hands it to the order with `LettBestilt.orderFields()`. Only newsletter subscribers are tracked, and the script sends nothing itself.
- `attributedCampaignId` in the `ORDER_CREATED` and `ORDER_PAID` webhooks: the campaign the order was credited to, or `null`.

## 2026-10-16: LettBestilt Partner API, scopes and sandbox payments

**Only additive changes in `/api/v1`.** No endpoint, field or event was removed or renamed. Existing keys keep full access, so integrations that work today keep working without changes.

### New

- **[Partner API](/partner-api)** under `/api/v1/partner`: list and create restaurants, read a restaurant's setup, delete a sandbox or archive a real restaurant, rotate and revoke restaurant keys, and create, list and delete webhooks. Server to server only.
- **Partner key** (`lbp_test_…`), created in the partner portal under **Settings → Partner key**. It only works against `/api/v1/partner`, together with the restaurant's own key in `X-LettBestilt-Restaurant-Key` for calls about one restaurant. Only test partner keys are available for now; they see and create sandboxes.
- **[Scopes](/authentication#scopes)** on restaurant keys. A key can be limited to some endpoints; a call outside them returns `403 INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` with `error.details.requiredScope`. Existing keys keep full access.
- **[Expiry dates](/authentication#expiry-dates)** on keys, from tomorrow up to two years ahead. An expired key returns `401 KEY_EXPIRED`. The partner portal warns 14 days before.
- **[Sandbox payments](/payments#testing-payments-in-a-sandbox).** In a sandbox, `stripeUrl` points to a LettBestilt test page where the payment is approved or cancelled. `ORDER_PAID` is sent as usual, and no money moves.
- **[Webhook delivery log](/webhooks#delivery-log)** in the partner portal, with **Resend** (same `X-LettBestilt-Delivery-Id`, so receivers deduplicate), pause and resume, and changing events. Deliveries are kept for 30 days.
- **[`PING` test event](/webhooks#test-event)**, sent from the partner portal and signed like every other event. You cannot subscribe to it, and it is only sent when you ask for it.
- **[API log](/debugging#the-api-log)** per restaurant in the partner portal: status, route, key and request ID of each call, kept for 14 days, searchable by request ID, including an `X-Request-Id` you send yourself. **Check integration** sums up keys, domains, webhooks, errors and return URLs for the last 24 hours.

### Changed

- Restaurant endpoints in `/api/v1` accept only restaurant keys. A partner key there is rejected with `401 UNAUTHORIZED`.
- New error codes: `KEY_EXPIRED`, `INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE`, and the partner API's `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REQUIRED`, `PARTNER_AGREEMENT_REQUIRED`, `SLUG_TAKEN`, `SANDBOX_LIMIT`, `RETENTION`, `RESTAURANT_ARCHIVED`, `KEY_NOT_FOUND`, `KEY_IN_USE`, `WEBHOOK_NOT_FOUND`, `WEBHOOK_LIMIT` and `CHANGED`. See [Errors](/errors).
- In a sandbox, `successUrl` and `cancelUrl` on `POST /orders` can be left out; the customer then returns to the test page.
