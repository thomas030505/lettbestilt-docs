LettBestilt runs online ordering for restaurants in Norway. This API is for agencies and developers who build restaurant websites: you design and host the site, and LettBestilt runs everything behind the order button.

| You build | LettBestilt runs |
|---|---|
| The website, its design and content | Menus, prices, opening hours and delivery zones |
| The menu pages, cart and checkout UI | Price calculation, coupons and promotions |
| The order confirmation and tracking page | Payment through SagaPay's hosted page, or cash |
| Your own integrations, through webhooks | The kitchen screen, tickets, customer emails and reservations |

The restaurant manages its menu, sold-out items, opening hours and rush mode in its LettBestilt dashboard. Your site reads the changes through the API, so you never hard-code menu data.

## What you can build

- **Menu pages** with categories, variants, add-ons, combo deals, allergens and the restaurant's own branding.
- **Checkout** for pickup and delivery, paid online (card, Vipps and the other methods the restaurant has on SagaPay) or in cash.
- **Order tracking** on your own site, with status and ready time.
- **Table reservations**: available times and reservation requests that the restaurant approves.
- **Coupons, automatic promotions and newsletter signup.**
- **Webhooks** to your own systems when orders and reservations change.
- **Restaurant management** from your own systems with the [partner API](/partner-api): create sandboxes, rotate keys and manage webhooks.

## The basics

Every request goes to one base URL:

```
https://www.lettbestilt.no/api/v1
```

<Warning>
Always use `www.lettbestilt.no`. The bare domain redirects to `www`, and the redirect drops the `Authorization` header.
</Warning>

Each restaurant is identified by its **slug**, which is also its LettBestilt subdomain: `pizzaroma` for `pizzaroma.lettbestilt.no`. Most endpoints take it as `?slug=pizzaroma`.

Calls from your server use the restaurant's **API key**. Calls from the browser are allowed from the restaurant's **verified domains**. See [Authentication](/authentication).

## What you get from LettBestilt

As a partner you get:

- sandboxes: test restaurants with test keys and simulated online payment, so you can build before a real restaurant exists
- a partner key for the [partner API](/partner-api)

Per restaurant you build for, you get:

- API keys, which you create, limit, rotate and revoke yourself in the partner portal
- webhook subscriptions to your endpoints, with a delivery log, test events and resend, also in the partner portal
- an API log and an integration check, so you can see why a call failed without asking us
- the domains your site runs on, verified for browser calls and payment return URLs

To start building on LettBestilt, [become a partner](https://www.lettbestilt.no/bli-partner).
