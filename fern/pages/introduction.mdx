LettBestilt is the ordering engine behind restaurant websites in Norway. You build the website; we run menus, orders, payments, kitchen tickets and reservations.

With the public API you can:

- **Show the live menu**: categories, products, variants, add-ons, allergens, opening hours and delivery zones.
- **Take orders** for pickup or delivery, paid online through a hosted payment page (card, Vipps and the other methods the restaurant has enabled) or in cash.
- **Track orders** with a public token, so customers can follow their order on your site.
- **Take table reservations** and check availability.
- **Validate coupons**, preview automatic promotions and sign customers up for the restaurant's newsletter.
- **Receive webhooks** when orders are created, paid, change status or are cancelled.

Everything the restaurant changes in its LettBestilt dashboard (prices, sold-out items, opening hours, rush mode) shows up through the API right away. Your site never needs to hard-code menu data.

## Base URL

```
https://www.lettbestilt.no/api/v1
```

<Warning>
Always use `www.lettbestilt.no`. The bare domain `lettbestilt.no` redirects to `www`, and redirects drop the `Authorization` header.
</Warning>

## How a restaurant is identified

Every request names the restaurant with the `slug` query parameter, for example `?slug=pizzaroma`. The slug is the restaurant's subdomain on LettBestilt (`pizzaroma.lettbestilt.no`).
